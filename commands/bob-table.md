---
name: bob-table
description: Mirror your introductions and people into an Airtable base you can sort, filter and share.
---

Run the `bob-table` skill. It copies `intros.csv` and `people.csv` into a base
the user owns, through their own Airtable connector.

Before doing anything, say the cost in one sentence:

> This copies your network to Airtable. It leaves your laptop.

If they decline, stop. The files are still theirs and nothing is lost.
